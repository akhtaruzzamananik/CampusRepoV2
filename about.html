What is Campus Repo?

Campus Repo is built around a simple idea: students should be able to find the resources they need without searching through countless groups, posts, links, and scattered platforms.

Students can discover academic playlists, question papers, and other useful learning resources based on their educational background.

At the same time, students can contribute resources and help others in their academic journey.
