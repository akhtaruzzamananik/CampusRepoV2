Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how StudyNest handles information associated with your use of the platform.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

StudyNest respects your privacy and is committed to handling your information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use StudyNest, why it may be used, and how it may be protected.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, StudyNest may collect information such as:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Country and study level.
  • Institution, department, program, or class information.
  • Resources or contributions submitted by you.
  • Saved playlists and question papers.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

3. Information from Google Login

If Google Sign-In is available and you choose to use it, StudyNest may receive basic account information provided through the authentication process, such as your name, email address, and profile information permitted by you.

Your academic information, such as country, study level, institution, and department, may still need to be provided separately to personalize your StudyNest experience.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by StudyNest may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Personalize academic resources and recommendations.
  • Display your profile and contributions.
  • Maintain your saved playlists and question papers.
  • Process and manage resource contributions.
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of StudyNest.
  • Respond to support requests and feedback.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the platform.

5. Academic Profile Information

StudyNest may ask for information such as your country, study level, institution, department, program, or class.

This information is primarily used to organize and personalize academic content for your educational context.

6. User Contributions

When you contribute a playlist, question paper, video, or other resource, some information associated with that contribution may be visible to other StudyNest users.

This may include your display name, contribution title, academic category, and the resource itself.

Do not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information as part of a public contribution unless you have the appropriate permission to share it.

7. Saved Content

StudyNest may store information about resources that you choose to save to your personal library, such as saved playlists and question papers.

Saved content is associated with your account so that you can access it again later.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

StudyNest may use cookies, browser storage, local storage, or similar technologies to support authentication, preferences, saved content, and other functionality.

The exact technologies used may change as the platform develops.

9. Third-Party Services

StudyNest may use third-party services for features such as authentication, video hosting, file storage, analytics, email delivery, or infrastructure.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with StudyNest.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take appropriate steps to protect their account credentials.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide StudyNest services, maintain accounts, comply with applicable requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain legitimate platform records.

12. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and the features available on StudyNest, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Accessing information associated with your account.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of certain information.
  • Managing information in your account settings.
  • Contacting us regarding privacy-related questions.

13. Children's Privacy

StudyNest is designed as an educational platform. Where applicable, users should meet the minimum age requirements imposed by applicable law and by the services used to access the platform.

If you believe that information belonging to a child has been collected improperly, please contact us.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when StudyNest's features, technologies, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page together with a revised "Last Updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact StudyNest.
