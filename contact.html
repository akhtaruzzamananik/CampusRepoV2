Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about contacting StudyNest.

How can I contribute a resource?

Go to the Contribute section from the sidebar and choose the type of resource you want to share.

I found an incorrect resource. What should I do?

Send us the details through the contact form so the resource can be reviewed.

Can I suggest a new feature?

Yes. We welcome suggestions that can improve the learning experience for students.

How can I get help with my account?

Send us a message with your issue and the email associated with your StudyNest account.
